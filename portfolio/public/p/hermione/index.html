Everything in one place

Plan it, write it, ship it.

The shapes you already know (a board, an issue, a page tree) rebuilt from scratch to be fast, keyboard-first and yours.

Board, backlog, sprints, roadmap.

Drag cards across your workflow, plan a sprint from the backlog, and see the quarter on a timeline.

  • Workflow rules decide what an issue needs before it moves.
  • Sprints with a burndown, velocity, and a close that asks where unfinished work goes.
  • Triage new work with single keys: A accept, D decline.
  • Releases and a roadmap built from your epics.

Planning guide →

Issues that keep their history.

Every change is recorded on the issue, every list is filterable, and nothing is ever more than a keystroke away.

  • Filters like assignee:me status:open priority:high.
  • Subtasks, links, a work log with a timer, attachments and comments.
  • Select rows to change status, assignee, priority or sprint at once.
  • Automations set priority, assign or label for you.

Issues guide →

A wiki that lives next to the work.

Write in Markdown or the Visual editor. Pages link to issues and to each other, and diagrams draw themselves.

  • Mermaid flowcharts and sequence diagrams, right in the page.
  • Live issue lists that update as work moves.
  • Templates: meeting notes, decision records, retros, briefs.
  • Backlinks and private pages. Export to Markdown or PDF.

Pages guide →

Know where things stand.

Reports that answer real questions, goals that connect the quarter to the work, and one page for every project.

  • Cycle and lead time, cumulative flow, created vs resolved, sprint reports.
  • Quarterly goals with linked epics and progress.
  • All projects on one page: open and overdue issues, and each sprint's progress.

Reports guide →

Built for the keyboard.

Jump anywhere with ⌘K, capture from any app, and find issues from macOS Spotlight.

  • Quick add understands bug: login fails p1 3pts friday.
  • Undo history (⇧⌘H) goes back several changes at once.
  • Import from Jira, Linear, Trello or GitHub; export everything.
  • 14 daily backups and a 30-day Trash.

The assistant · ⌘J

Just say it.

Type what you want the way you'd tell a teammate. Hermione makes the change, tells you what it did, and puts Undo under every answer.

Move HER-2 to done, make HER-5 urgent and give it to Priya
Done. HER-2 is in Done. HER-5 is now urgent and assigned to Priya.
↶ Undo this
What's left in the sprint?
Four issues, 11 points left, with two days to go. Two are in review.
01 · INSTANT

A built-in engine

Hand-written rules read keys, statuses, people, dates and points. It answers offline, in milliseconds, and needs no download.

02 · ON YOUR MAC

A model, when it's needed

Looser requests go to Hermione AI running on 127.0.0.1, or to your own llama.cpp, Ollama or LM Studio.

03 · SAFE

A plan for big changes

More than five changes shows a plan first. Archiving and closing a sprint always ask. Every answer can be undone.

Hermione AI · 2B

A small model, trained for one job.

Qwen3.5-2B, fine-tuned with QLoRA on 30,000 generated conversations about Hermione's own tools, then quantised to run on any Apple-silicon Mac. Scored on 500 requests it never saw.

Under the hood

One path for every change.
