Using Hermione

Getting around

#Home

G then H. What needs you today: your open issues, what's due this week, recently edited pages, the running sprint, and the project's shape by status, type, priority and person.

#My day

G then D. A short list you pick each morning. Add issues from the suggestions (due soon, in progress, assigned to you), drag them into the order you'll do them, and tick them off. It keeps until tomorrow.

#Inbox

G then N. Mentions of you, comments on issues you watch, and work assigned to you, newest first. The red count in the sidebar is what you haven't read.

#Search

⌘K (or /) searches every issue, page and comment, and every command.

  • Type an issue key (HER-12), words from a title or a description, or a person's name.
  • Filter the results to Issues, Pages or Views with the chips at the top.
  • Commands are searchable by what they do: "dark", "density", "new sprint", "export". Settings are too: "licence" opens Settings → Plan.
  • What you've opened recently shows first when the box is empty.

#Spotlight

Issues and pages show up in macOS Spotlight (⌘Space) by key and title, kept current as you work. Choosing one opens it in Hermione. Turn it off in Settings → Privacy; that also removes everything Hermione added to the index.

#Quick capture

⇧⌘Space from any app opens a small Hermione window. Type an issue in one line with shorthand, press Return, and it lands in Triage without leaving what you were doing:

bug: Fix the export button friday p1 @noah #web 3pts
Shorthand Sets
bug: task: story: epic: at the start The type
p1 … p4, or !!! !! The priority (p1 is highest)
@noah, @me The assignee
#web A label
3pts, 3 points The estimate
today, friday, next week, in 3 days, dec 3 The due date

Whatever is left becomes the summary. The same shorthand works in Add issue on the board and in ⌘K.

#Reminders and the daily digest

  • The Mac app reminds you about issues due today or late: once shortly after it opens, then every hour, never more than once a day for the same issue.
  • Daily digest (off until you turn it on, Settings → You): once a day after the hour you choose, one notification with what's due, what's late, what's unread and what's left in the sprint. It's worked out on your Mac; a day with nothing to say is skipped.

#Undo history

⌘Z undoes the last change and ⇧⌘Z redoes it. ⇧⌘H opens the history: the last 30 changes in plain words ("Moved HER-4 to Done"). Click any of them to go back to just before it, several changes at once; click again to go forward.

#The sidebar

[ collapses it. ⌘[ and ⌘] go back and forward. The numbers beside each view are counts of what's in it. Your saved views, the project switcher and the sprint progress live here too.
