Using Hermione

Planning

Everything under Planning in the sidebar works on the same issues: move a card on the board and it has moved in the backlog, the sprint, the roadmap and the reports.

#Board

G then B. The active sprint, one column per status in your workflow.

  • Drag a card to another column or another place in its column. The toast that follows has Undo, and ⌘Z works too.
  • Drag from the keyboard: focus a card, press Space to pick it up, the arrow keys to move it, Space to drop it, Esc to put it back.
  • Click a card to open it in a side panel. ⌘-click opens the full issue.
  • Add issue at the foot of a column creates one already in that status.
  • Filter by person (the avatars), type, label, or the search box. Only my issues shows yours.
  • Swimlanes: group the board by assignee or by epic.
  • Work-in-progress limits: click a column's count to set a limit. The column turns red when it holds more than that.

#Several cards at once

Shift-click or ⌘-click cards, or press X on a focused card, to select more than one. A bar appears: move, assign, set the priority, or move them all to the Trash. Drag one selected card and the rest come with it.

#Right-click

Right-click any card (or press Shift+F10 on it) for its status, priority, assign to me, copy key, copy link, archive and Trash.

#Backlog

G then L. Everything not in a running sprint, ranked. Drag rows to change the order; the top is what you'd do next. Drag issues into a planned sprint at the top of the page, or select several and pick a sprint.

#Sprints

G then S. Plan a sprint, start it, finish it.

  1. Create a sprint with a name, a goal and a length (one to six weeks).
  2. Fill it by dragging issues in from the backlog. The header adds up the points as you go, against your average velocity from past sprints.
  3. Start sprint. Its issues appear on the board.
  4. During the sprint, the burndown shows the points left against an even line to zero, and the ring in the board header shows points done.
  5. Complete sprint. Choose where unfinished issues go: the backlog or the next sprint. Hermione keeps a sprint report: what was finished, what was carried over, what was added after it started.

The assistant can do most of this: "plan the next sprint with 20 points" fills it by priority until the points run out, "start the next sprint", "how is the sprint going?". See The assistant.

#Roadmap

G then R. Epics on a timeline. Drag a bar to move it, drag its ends to change its start or end. Each bar fills in as its child issues are done.

#Releases

G then E. Group the issues that ship together under a version, see how many are done, and mark it released on the day. The assistant can write the release notes from those issues: "draft release notes".

#Calendar

G then C. A month with every issue on its due date. Move between months, or jump back with Today.

#Workload

G then W. Open points per person, so you can see who has too much before you plan the next sprint. Click a person to see their issues.

#Triage

New issues from quick capture and imports land in Triage first. Move through them with J and K, then:

Key Does
A Accept into the backlog
M Assign to me and accept
S Snooze for a week
D Decline and archive

Nothing in triage counts toward sprints or reports until it's accepted.

#Several projects

Each project has its own key (WEB, APP) that prefixes its issues (WEB-12), its own workflow, people, labels and fields. Switch projects from the card at the top of the sidebar, or start one with New project. All projects, in the same menu, shows every project's open and overdue issues and sprint progress on one page.

Give a project an icon and a colour in Settings → Project. Archive a project you've finished; it stays searchable and can be restored.
