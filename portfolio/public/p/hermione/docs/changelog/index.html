#0.3.0 · November 2026

  • Hermione AI on your Mac. Pro can download Hermione's own model and run the assistant without the internet. Pause and resume big downloads any time.
  • Web answers. Hermione AI + Web can look things up for you. Only the search words leave your Mac.
  • A tidier Settings. Sections for you, your plan, the assistant, each project, privacy and this Mac.
  • Examples in one click. Empty lists offer realistic examples to try things out. One Undo removes them.
  • Copy diagnostics. Settings → About builds a short report for bug reports, without any of your project text.
  • Find it from Spotlight. Issues and pages show up in macOS search and open straight in Hermione. Turn it off in Settings → Privacy.
  • Visual editing and diagrams. Every page has a Visual tab, and mermaid flowcharts and sequence diagrams are drawn right in the page.
  • Reports, goals and all projects. Cycle time, cumulative flow, quarterly goals with linked epics, and one page with every project's progress.
  • Move in and move out. Import from Jira, Linear, Trello or GitHub. Export everything as Markdown, or a page as a PDF.
  • Rules that do the busywork. Workflow rules decide what an issue needs before it moves; automations set priority, assign or label for you.

Under the hood: the Mac app now bundles llama-server (llama.cpp b12823) with only the libraries it links, so the download is about 20 MB.

#0.2.0 · November 2026

  • A fresh look. New colours, type and motion, with light and dark themes and your choice of accent.
  • Welcome. A short setup that asks your name and what you do, then picks a starting project.
