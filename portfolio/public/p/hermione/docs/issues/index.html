Using Hermione

Issues

An issue is one piece of work: a bug, a task, a story, an epic that holds others, or a subtask. Its key, like HER-12, is the project key plus a number, and stays the same forever.

#Creating one

  • Press C anywhere, or New issue on Home.
  • Add issue at the foot of a board column.
  • ⌘J and say it: "create a bug: login fails on Safari, high priority, for @priya, due friday".
  • ⇧⌘Space from any app on your Mac opens quick capture.
  • From a page: a to-do line in a description can become a child issue with one click.

Issue templates (Bug, Feature, Chore) fill the description with the usual headings.

#Fields

Every field edits in place, saves at once, and shows up in History.

Field What it's for
Type Epic, story, task, bug or subtask, each with its own colour and icon
Status Where it is in your workflow
Priority Highest, high, medium, low, lowest
Assignee One person; Assign to me is one click
Reporter Who raised it
Points An estimate, used by sprints, velocity and workload
Due date Shown on cards, the calendar and Home; late issues turn red
Sprint Which sprint it's in, or the backlog
Labels Coloured tags; type # to add one
Parent The epic or story it belongs to
Release The version it ships in
Custom fields Text, number, choice or date fields you add in Settings

When the assistant sets a field, a small mark beside it says so, with the sentence that did it.

#Child issues

Child issues list the work inside an epic or story as a checklist. Add one inline, tick it done, drag to reorder, or turn a - [ ] line in the description into a child issue. The parent shows how many are done.

#Links

Link issues that block each other, relate to each other or duplicate each other. Both sides show the link. Blocked issues show a warning on the board.

#Comments

Markdown, @mentions (the person sees it in their Inbox), files, and reactions on any comment. Vote on an issue to show it matters. Watch an issue to hear about every change.

#Time

Work log keeps an original estimate, time spent and time left. Start a timer from the issue; it shows in the sidebar while it runs and logs the time when you stop it.

#History and backlinks

History lists every change: who, what, from what, to what, when. Pages and other issues that mention this one show under Linked from, so you can see where it's discussed.

#Archive, Trash and delete

  • Archive hides an issue from lists and the board; it stays searchable and comes back with Restore.
  • Delete moves it to the Trash for 30 days.

#The issue list

G then I. Every issue in the project as a table.

  • Sort by any column, group by status, assignee, priority, epic, label or sprint.
  • Select rows to change status, assignee, priority or sprint for all of them at once.
  • Save a view with its filters, sort and grouping. Saved views show in the sidebar.
  • Filter with the menus, or type a filter into the box:
Type Finds
assignee:me or @me Your issues
status:open, is:done Open or finished issues
type:bug, priority:high or p1 By type or priority
label:web or #web By label
sprint:current, sprint:none In the running sprint, or in no sprint
due<7d, due:today, is:late Due soon, due today, overdue
points>3, points:none By estimate
updated<7d Changed this week
-type:epic Anything, with a - in front, leaves those out
"login page" Words in quotes stay together

Combine them: is:open assignee:me due<7d -type:epic. The same filters work in live issue lists on pages.
