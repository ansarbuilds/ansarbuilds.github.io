Hermione Pro

Hermione is free and open source, and every feature in the app is free forever. Hermione Pro (CA$10 a month or CA$100 a year) pays for the things that cost money to make and run, and adds them on top.

Free, forever Pro
Every feature: board, backlog, sprints, roadmap, releases, pages, reports, goals Hermione AI: the trained model, downloaded in one click and run on your Mac
The built-in assistant (⌘J) Hermione AI + Web: also looks things up; only the search words leave your Mac
Your own model (llama.cpp, Ollama, LM Studio), including Hermione's open weights Sync between Mac, iPad and iPhone, end-to-end encrypted (coming)
Import, export, backups, your data folder Priority support

#Getting Pro

Online checkout is being set up. Until it opens, Get Pro in Settings → Plan leads here; get in touch through ansarbuilds.dev for a key.

#How a licence works

A licence is a signed key that looks like HERMIONE-eyJ2Ijox….Fh3k….

  1. Settings → Plan → Have a licence key? Paste it and press Activate.
  2. Hermione checks the key's signature on your Mac. No account, no internet, nothing sent anywhere.
  3. Pro features switch on.

Each key has an end date: monthly plans get a fresh key each month, yearly plans each year. If a renewal is late, Pro keeps working for 14 more days.

#Promises

  • Your data is never held hostage. If Pro ends, every project, page and backup stays and keeps working. Only the Pro features switch off.
  • Never paywalled: your data, export, import, backups, the full app, working offline, using your own model, and building Hermione from source.
  • Still open source. All the code, including the Pro features, is Apache-2.0. Anyone may build their own copy. What Pro buys is the trained models delivered and kept up to date, the hosted services, and the people behind them. The name and logo are trademarks; forks should use their own.

#Why charge for an open-source app?

GPU time to train the models, hosting the downloads, a search relay, the sync relay, Apple's developer fee for signed builds, and the time to build all of it. It's the same deal as Obsidian Sync or Zed Pro.
