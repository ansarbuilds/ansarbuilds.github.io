Using Hermione

Reports and goals

#Reports

G then T. How long work takes and how it flows, worked out from every issue's history. Pick a window of 14, 30, 90 or 180 days.

Number or chart Means
Median cycle time From an issue starting (first moved into a started status) to finishing
Median lead time From an issue being created to finishing
Issues finished / opened In the window
Cumulative flow Issues in each status category, day by day. A widening middle band means work starts faster than it finishes
Created vs resolved New issues against finished ones, day by day

Every chart has Show as a table for the numbers behind it, and the colours are checked for colour blindness in both themes.

A median shows a dash until at least one issue has both started and finished in the window.

#Sprint reports

Each completed sprint keeps a report on the Sprints page: what was committed, what was finished, what was carried over and what was added after the start, with its burndown. Velocity compares the points finished across your recent sprints. The assistant can turn a sprint report into a retro page: "draft a retro for the last sprint".

#Goals

G then O. Quarterly goals, each linked to the epics that deliver it. A goal's progress is the share of its epics' issues that are done, so it moves as the work does, with nothing to update by hand.

  1. Add goal, with a title and the quarter.
  2. Link an epic from the goal's card.
  3. Watch it fill.

#All projects

The project menu → All projects: every project on one page with its open and overdue issues and the running sprint's progress. Click one to open it.
