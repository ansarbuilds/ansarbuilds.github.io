How it works

No hotkey. Just look and talk.

The camera and the microphone have to agree before saywhen listens. A camera alone cannot tell chewing from talking. A microphone alone cannot tell who you are talking to.

You stay in control

Four ways to send. All cancelable.

⏸

Just pause

Stop talking. A short countdown runs, then it sends.

default
↓

Nod to send

Nothing goes until you nod. Shake your head to drop it.

“”

Say "send it"

End with "send it" to press Enter. Otherwise it is only typed.

⌨

Type only

Your words are typed and Enter is never pressed.

best first try

Cancel any time with Esc, a head shake, or by talking again. Or say "scratch that".

Private by design

It sees a few numbers. Never a picture.

Everything runs on your computer. After the speech model downloads, saywhen works with the internet off.

Illustrations drawn from the app's interface, not recordings.

The camera

Each frame is read for a moment, then thrown away. Nothing is saved, logged or uploaded.

Your voice

Speech becomes text on this computer with Whisper, then the audio is discarded. Cloud clean-up is off unless you turn it on.

The Enter key

Pressed only in apps you trust. Commands like rm -rf are typed with a warning and left for you. That check is a heuristic, not a guarantee.

About the install warning. This beta is not signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, so your computer asks one extra question. It is about the missing signature. You can also build saywhen yourself from the source.

cd saywhen
npm ci
npx tauri dev
