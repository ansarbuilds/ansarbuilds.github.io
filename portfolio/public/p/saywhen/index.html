Look. Talk. Sent.

saywhen is hands-free voice input for terminals and coding assistants. Look at the screen and say what you want. It types it, shows you a short preview you can cancel, and then presses Enter. No hotkey to hold.

How it works

Attention replaces the hotkey.

The webcam and the microphone have to agree before saywhen listens. Neither is enough alone: a camera cannot tell chewing from talking, and a microphone cannot tell who you are talking to.

01 · LOOK

Face the screen

The camera notices you are facing the screen. It keeps a few numbers, such as head angle. Pictures are never saved or sent.

02 · TALK

Just speak

When your lips move and the microphone hears a voice, it starts listening. The last half second is kept, so the first word is not lost.

03 · CHECK

See it first

Speech becomes text on your computer. dash dash release becomes --release. A preview shows the result with a short countdown.

04 · SENT

Then Enter

The text is typed into the app in front. Enter is pressed only in apps you trust. Press Esc, shake your head, or keep talking to cancel.

You stay in control

Four ways to send. All of them cancelable.

Just pause

Stop talking. A short countdown runs, then it sends. The default.

Nod to send

Nothing is sent until you nod.

Say "send it"

End with "send it" to press Enter. Otherwise the text is only typed.

Type only

Your words are typed. Enter is never pressed. Best for a first try.

TRUSTED APPS

Enter only where it is safe

Terminals and code editors are trusted by default. In chat, email and browsers, saywhen only types.

RISKY COMMANDS

Never sent for you

Commands such as rm -rf and git push --force are typed with a warning. You press Enter yourself. The check is a heuristic, not a guarantee.

PRIVATE

It stays on your computer

Camera frames live in memory for a moment. Audio is discarded after it becomes text. After the speech model is downloaded, it works offline.

The short animations on this page are illustrations drawn from the app's interface. They are not screen recordings, and their timing is not a measurement.

Safe to try

The camera, the microphone, and Enter.

Those are the three things worth asking about before you install an app like this.

The camera

Each frame is used for a moment to notice your face, then thrown away. Nothing is saved, and nothing is uploaded.

Your voice

Speech becomes text on this computer. The audio is discarded after that. Cloud clean-up stays off until you turn it on.

Enter

A preview comes first. Press Esc, shake your head, or keep talking to cancel. Type only never presses Enter. Start there.

The warning

This beta is not signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, so Mac and Windows ask an extra question. That question is about the missing signature. You can also build saywhen from the public source and skip the downloaded app.
